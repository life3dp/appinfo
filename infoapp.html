SpeedEye Privacy Policy

Summary
  • SpeedEye does not collect or transmit any personal data to the developer.
  • Location is used and stored only on your iPhone to power the speedometer and trip records.
  • No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

1. Location

  • SpeedEye uses your iPhone’s location (GPS) to calculate current speed, distance, route, and average/top speed.
  • The app requests “While Using the App” permission only (it never asks for “Always”).
  • Only after you tap “Start” on a trip, the app keeps receiving location in the background until you “Arrive,” so the trip continues if the screen turns off or you switch apps (iOS shows a location indicator). Location is not used in the background when you are not on a trip.
  • Speedometer Mode receives location only while its screen is visible and records nothing.
  • Trip records (including routes) are stored only in the app’s on-device storage and are never sent to the developer or any server.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Trips: start/arrival times, driving and stopped time, distance, average/top speed, route, speed chart, and—if you turn it on—estimated fuel efficiency, fuel use, and fuel cost.
  • Settings: speedometer design, units, language, Speedometer Mode, and “My Vehicle” details (vehicle name, fuel type, rated efficiency, fuel price).
  • This data stays on your device; the developer cannot see it.

3. Network connections

  • SpeedEye does not exchange any data with the developer or any external server. Fuel price is entered by you or uses a default value.
  • Maps are displayed with Apple Maps (MapKit), subject to Apple’s privacy policy.

4. Sharing, advertising, analytics

We do not share or sell personal data. The app contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash reporting.

5. Your controls and deletion

  • Change location access anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › SpeedEye.
  • Delete a trip by swiping it in the Trip History list; its route data is deleted with it.
  • Deleting the app removes all data stored on the device.

6. Children

SpeedEye is not directed to children and collects no personal data from anyone, so there is no children’s data to share.

7. Changes

Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future feature changes how data is handled (for example, iCloud sync), this policy will be updated first.

8. Contact

Please use the email address in the Support section below.

My OX Quiz Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-08 (Korea Standard Time) · Developer life3dp · Version 1.0

Summary
  • Topics and questions are stored on this device. life3dp does not run a server that receives your quizzes.
  • Private iCloud save is off by default. It sends requests only after you turn it on and confirm, and only for the same Apple Account.
  • Turning it off stops later requests only. It does not delete records already in private iCloud.
  • No app sign-up, no developer backend, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics SDK, no tracking domains, no public sharing, and no online play.
  • If you email support, the developer can receive what you send. This policy does not state that no data is collected.

1. What stays on the device

  • Topic names, question text, and the O or X you mark correct in 마이퀴즈 (Custom Quiz) are stored in the app on this device.
  • Round progress—who stood on which side, and who is still in—stays in memory for that round and is not sent to the developer. A round already started keeps the questions it copied at the start.
  • When the topic list is empty the first time, a bundled sample of 10 questions is prepared on this device. That sample is not sent to a developer server and does not create a new analytics or tracking transmission.
  • Play is on one shared device, for 1 to 10 people. Anyone using that device can see the quiz on the screen.

2. Optional private iCloud

  • The container is Apple’s CloudKit private database iCloud.com.life3dp.fatechoice. The app does not use a public database. It does not publish your questions to other people or play them online with other devices.
  • “Save in private iCloud” on My quizzes starts off. Turning it on asks you to confirm. Cancel leaves it off. That choice stays on this device.
  • While it is on, topic, question, and deletion records can be saved to the private iCloud of the same Apple Account. Those records can include topic names, question text, and the correct O or X. If private iCloud is on, sample questions you keep on the device can be included in the same way as other quiz text.
  • This save needs an iCloud sign-in on the device, a network connection, and Apple’s CloudKit. Data Apple hosts is subject to Apple’s policies.
  • Until the app says the topics are saved in private iCloud, do not treat them as stored there.
  • Turning the switch off stops later CloudKit requests, leaves topics on this device, and does not delete records already in private iCloud. This app has no button that deletes those cloud records.
  • The CloudKit user-record name is stored only on this device so these topics are not uploaded to a different Apple Account and are not replaced by another account’s quizzes. That value is not sent to the developer and is not a life3dp app account.

3. Who can see a quiz

  • life3dp does not operate a server that receives quizzes and does not sell them.
  • Records saved to private iCloud are in that user’s Apple Account private database. This policy does not say that nobody can access data Apple hosts. It does not promise end-to-end encryption.
  • The app has no feature that gathers every user’s private quizzes for the developer. A developer signed into Apple’s CloudKit console can view the private data of the iCloud account used in that console. That is not a directory of every player, and it is not a promise that private data of an account used there can never be opened.
  • CloudKit diagnostic logs record the operation, domain, and error code only. They do not record question text or user-record names. There is no analytics SDK.

4. What the app does not request

The app does not ask for location, microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or notifications. Sound and music are files inside the app and play on the device. The app does not record your voice. There are no ads, in-app purchases, analytics or tracking SDKs, tracking domains, app sign-up, developer backend, public quiz packs, or online play.

5. Deletion

  • Deleting a question in the app removes that question from this device. Deleting a topic also removes that topic’s questions from this device. They are not moved to another topic.
  • A round already started can continue with the copy it made at the start.
  • Deleting the app removes the quiz stored on the device and the sync note that existed only on the device. Uninstalling does not guarantee deletion of records already saved in private iCloud.
  • Records that remain are in that Apple Account’s iCloud and can be handled with the iCloud controls Apple provides for the account. This app does not include that deletion screen. The developer cannot promise to delete your private CloudKit records for you, and this policy promises no retention period.

Email you choose to send to feelfineart@gmail.com for support can be received by the developer. The app does not collect a phone number or a home address, and this page does not publish either. Do not include quiz text you want kept private unless you mean to share it in that email.

기기 한 대를 돌려 가며 1명에서 10명까지 플레이합니다. 온라인 대전은 없습니다. 같은 기기를 쓰는 사람은 화면에 나온 문제를 볼 수 있습니다.

iCloud 없이 할 수 있나요?

할 수 있습니다. 개인 iCloud 저장은 선택이며 기본은 꺼져 있습니다. 켜지 않아도 랜덤 O/X와 이 기기의 마이퀴즈를 할 수 있습니다. 켜려면 확인이 필요하고, 끄면 이후 요청만 멈추며 이미 올라간 개인 iCloud 기록을 지우지는 않습니다.

퀴즈를 지우려면 어떻게 하나요?

앱에서 문제를 지우면 이 기기의 그 문제가 지워집니다. 주제를 지우면 그 주제의 문제도 이 기기에서 함께 지워집니다. 앱을 삭제하면 기기에 저장된 퀴즈는 지워집니다. 이미 개인 iCloud에 저장된 기록은 그 Apple 계정에 남을 수 있습니다. 그 기록은 Apple이 계정에 제공하는 iCloud 관리 수단으로 다룰 수 있습니다. 이 앱에는 그 클라우드 기록을 지우는 버튼이 없고, 개발자가 대신 지워 준다고 약속하지 않습니다.

광고, 결제, 가입이 있나요?

없습니다. 광고, 앱 내 구매, 앱 회원가입, 온라인 대전이 없습니다.

English
I see questions the first time I open Custom Quiz. What is the 10-question sample?

When the Custom Quiz list is empty the first time, a bundled sample of 10 questions is prepared on this device. That sample is not sent to a developer server and does not create a new analytics or tracking transmission. Private iCloud stays off unless you turn it on. If you later turn that save on, sample questions you keep can be included in that Apple Account’s private iCloud along with other quiz text.

How many people can play?

Pass one device around. One to ten people can play on that device. There is no online play. People using the same device can see the questions on the screen.

Can I play without iCloud?

Yes. Private iCloud save is optional and off by default. Random O/X and Custom Quiz on this device work without turning it on. Turning it on asks you to confirm. Turning it off stops later requests and does not delete private iCloud records already saved.

How do I delete a quiz?

Deleting a question removes it from this device. Deleting a topic also removes that topic’s questions from this device. Deleting the app removes the quiz stored on the device. Records already saved in private iCloud can remain in that Apple Account. You can handle them with the iCloud controls Apple provides for the account. This app has no button that deletes those cloud records, and the developer cannot promise to delete them for you.

Are there ads, purchases, or an account?

No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no app sign-up, and no online play.
