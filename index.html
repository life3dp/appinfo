SpeedEye Privacy Policy

Summary
  • SpeedEye does not collect or transmit any personal data to the developer.
  • Location is used and stored only on your iPhone to power the speedometer and trip records.
  • No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

1. Location

  • SpeedEye uses your iPhone’s location (GPS) to calculate current speed, distance, route, and average/top speed.
  • The app requests “While Using the App” permission only (it never asks for “Always”).
  • Only after you tap “Start” on a trip, the app keeps receiving location in the background until you “Arrive,” so the trip continues if the screen turns off or you switch apps (iOS shows a location indicator). Location is not used in the background when you are not on a trip.
  • Speedometer Mode receives location only while its screen is visible and records nothing.
  • Trip records (including routes) are stored only in the app’s on-device storage and are never sent to the developer or any server.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Trips: start/arrival times, driving and stopped time, distance, average/top speed, route, speed chart, and—if you turn it on—estimated fuel efficiency, fuel use, and fuel cost.
  • Settings: speedometer design, units, language, Speedometer Mode, and “My Vehicle” details (vehicle name, fuel type, rated efficiency, fuel price).
  • This data stays on your device; the developer cannot see it.

3. Network connections

  • When the app language is Korean, the app downloads the national average fuel price once a day from the public API of Korea National Oil Corporation’s Opinet (opinet.co.kr) to pre-fill the fuel price. No location, trip, vehicle, or device-identifying data is sent. As with any internet request, Opinet’s server may receive your IP address, subject to Opinet’s own policies. This feature is not used in other languages.
  • Maps are displayed with Apple Maps (MapKit), subject to Apple’s privacy policy.

4. Sharing, advertising, analytics

We do not share or sell personal data. The app contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash reporting.

5. Your controls and deletion

  • Change location access anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › SpeedEye.
  • Delete a trip by swiping it in the Trip History list; its route data is deleted with it.
  • Deleting the app removes all data stored on the device.

6. Children

SpeedEye is not directed to children and collects no personal data from anyone, so there is no children’s data to share.

7. Changes

Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future feature changes how data is handled (for example, iCloud sync), this policy will be updated first.

8. Contact

Please use the email address in the Support section below.

한국어
데이터를 백업하거나 다른 iPhone으로 옮길 수 있나요?

세 앱에는 서버나 계정, 자체 내보내기·동기화 기능이 없습니다. 데이터는 앱 안에만 저장되므로, iPhone 전체 백업(iCloud 백업 또는 컴퓨터 백업)을 켜 두었다면 iOS 백업에 앱 데이터가 함께 포함될 수 있습니다. 이 백업은 사용자의 Apple 계정에서 관리되며 개발자는 접근할 수 없습니다.

데이터를 지우려면 어떻게 하나요?

각 항목은 목록에서 왼쪽으로 밀어 삭제할 수 있습니다(스피드아이: 운행 기록, 생신언제지: 기념일, 몇살이지: 사람). 앱을 삭제하면 그 앱이 기기에 저장한 모든 데이터가 함께 삭제됩니다. 개발자는 데이터를 가지고 있지 않으므로 따로 삭제를 요청하실 필요가 없습니다.

생신언제지의 음력 날짜는 무엇을 기준으로 하나요?

한국천문연구원(KASI)의 음양력 자료를 기준으로 한 표를 앱에 내장해 기기 안에서 계산합니다. 한국 음력은 한국표준시(UTC+9) 기준이라 중국 달력과 하루 차이가 날 수 있습니다.

몇살이지의 학년은 어떻게 계산하나요?

한국 학제(3월 1일 학년도 시작, 초등 입학 = 출생 연도 + 7)를 기준으로 계산합니다. 조기 입학·입학 유예는 반영되지 않습니다.

스피드아이가 화면이 꺼져도 위치를 쓰나요?

‘시작하기’로 운행을 시작한 뒤 ‘도착’할 때까지만 백그라운드에서 위치를 받습니다. 운행 중이 아닐 때는 사용하지 않으며, 위치 정보는 기기 밖으로 전송되지 않습니다.

광고나 유료 기능이 있나요?

없습니다. 세 앱 모두 완전 무료이며 광고, 앱 내 구매, 회원가입이 없습니다.

How can I back up my data or move it to another iPhone?

The apps have no server, no account, and no built-in export or sync. Because data is stored inside the app, it may be included in your regular iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have that turned on. Those backups are managed by your Apple Account; the developer has no access to them.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe left on an item in the list to delete it (SpeedEye: trips, BirthdayWhen: dates, HowOld: people). Deleting an app deletes all data it stored on the device. The developer holds none of your data, so there is nothing else to request.

What are BirthdayWhen’s lunar dates based on?

A built-in table based on lunar–solar data from the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute (KASI), calculated on the device. The Korean lunar calendar uses Korea Standard Time (UTC+9), so it can differ from the Chinese calendar by a day.

How does HowOld calculate school grade?

It follows the Korean school system (school year starts March 1; elementary entry = birth year + 7). Early or delayed school entry is not reflected.

Does SpeedEye use my location when the screen is off?

Only between tapping “Start” and “Arrive” on a trip. Otherwise it does not use location in the background, and location never leaves your device.

Are there ads or paid features?

No. All three apps are completely free, with no ads, in-app purchases, or accounts.
